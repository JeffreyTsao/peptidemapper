Minigene Peptide Mapper

Maps peptides from a Proteome Discoverer export onto annotated SnapGene or GenBank constructs by six-frame translation, then flags which minigenes were covered and which peptides cross an AAY linker junction.

Proteome Discoverer .xlsx (nested or flat), or CSV/TSV
SnapGene .dna, or GenBank .gb / .gbk
